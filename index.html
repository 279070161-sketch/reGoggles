Open Smart Goggles For Industrial AI Assistance

Currently in development. Product design and features are subject to change before final release.

Sees What the Workers See, Enables What They Couldn't

Purpose-built for field technicians — an AI-native protective eyewear that makes on-site work smarter, faster, and fully data-driven.

First-Person AI Visual Inspection

  • First-person camera catches easily-missed details, improving detection consistency.

  • Flags deviations in real time, preventing defects from flowing downstream.

  • AI visual inspection complements human QC, removing operator-to-operator variation.

  • Every defect is traceable back to the exact SOP step.

Data-Based Performance Improvement

  • Verify root causes against real footage, not faded memories.

  • First-person data clearly shows real work details for analysis.

  • AI auto-tags key events and anomalies — engineers spend less time scrubbing through footage.

  • Video, audio, and motion on one synchronized timeline — nothing is taken out of context.

On-Site PPE Monitoring

  • Real-time PPE checks flag violations, reducing injuries.

  • Posture and movement data captured for ergonomic analysis.

  • Proximity alerts fire when workers approach unguarded machinery or hazardous zones.

  • First-person incident replay supports safety analysis.

Hands-Free Expertise Copilot

  • Expert operations are auto-captured and stored in a searchable knowledge base.

  • Recorded sessions can be turned into SOPs for new hires.

  • Workers get AI feedback hands-free, on the floor.

  • Recorded operations become training data that improves your models over time.

Live Streaming Remote Collaboration

  • First-person video streams to remote experts for guidance.

  • Built-in mics and speakers enable real-time two-way voice calls.

  • AI auto-summarizes remote guidance calls into searchable notes.

  • Guidance sessions are recorded and replayable anytime.

Real-Time Process Evidence Recording

  • One-click record retrieval for any batch speeds up audits.

  • Every batch is linked to its station, operator, and time.

  • SOP deviations auto-flagged, enabling faster corrective action loops.

  • On-premises storage keeps sensitive data within your infrastructure for data privacy requirements.

Ego Data for Embodied AI Training

  • Synchronized video, audio, and IMU streams form high-quality training datasets for embodied AI.

  • First-person footage of real frontline operations bridges the sim-to-real gap.

  • Hands-free goggles capture data passively while workers do their jobs.

  • Turns everyday frontline work into a scalable data engine for embodied AI.
