Define Your Sensing System with Versatile Expansion

Expansion ports support ToF, IMU, IR and more — free experience with different sensor setups.

01

Original features

The reGoggles itself comes with a built-in HD camera, microphones X3, speakers X2, Wi-Fi + BLE connectivity, and an IMU sensor.

02

General Expansion Port

The General Expansion Port supports ToF, optical modules, SD cards, environmental sensors, custom modules

03

Vision Expansion Port

The Vision Expansion Port supports additional Camera, additional Camera + IMU, and other vision module expansions.
