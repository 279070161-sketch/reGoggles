Open Smart Goggles For Industrial AI Assistance

Currently in development. Product design and features are subject to change before final release.

Sees What the Workers See, Enables What They Couldn't

Unlike industrial cameras, reGoggles captures, records, and streams the first-person perspective of field technicians, delivering the data to AI models or remote experts for analysis, which enables advance warning, real-time recording, and post-event optimization.

First-Person Inspection

Automated visual inspection isn't suited for manual assembly lines. Manual inspection varies from person to person and can miss critical defects. reGoggles brings AI-assisted quality inspection to the line, boosting yield.

  • HD Camera Spots Subtle Defects

  • Real-time AI Inspection

  • Step-Level Defect Traceability

Data-Based Improvement

Production planning relies on line-team experience and habit, and improvements often take weeks without data-backed evidence. reGoggles’ full-process first-person recordings let AI quantify operations and recommend concrete improvements.

  • Data-Driven Frontline Analysis

  • Rapid Retrospectives

  • Anomalies Timeline Pinpoint

On-Site PPE Monitoring

PPE and hazard detection are critical in chemical and construction industries. Manual safety checks cannot provide 24/7 coverage, leaving recurring gaps in safety coverage. Replacing traditional goggles, reGoggles provides continuous PPE checks and round-the-clock monitoring.

  • Monitoring While Working

  • Real-time Danger Alert

  • First-person Incident Replay and Liability Determination

Hands-Free Copilot

Expert visits are costly, and workers’ hands are typically occupied on the job. reGoggles streams first-person views for real-time remote support from experts or AI, without interrupting the work.

  • Operations Auto-Captured into SOPs

  • Hands-free AI Guidance

  • Remote Expert Collaboration

Evidence Recording

Compliance audits in petrochemical and other regulated industries demand airtight process evidence, yet on-site records rarely stand up to scrutiny. Workers capture process evidence in the field with reGoggles, closing the evidence loop while AI verifies compliance in real time.

  • Auto-linked to workstation, operator and shift

  • Open Data Pipelines with Local-First Security

  • Real-Time Recording with AI Verification

Ego Data Capture

First-person operation data from real industrial settings is invaluable for training embodied AI. Conventional ego-capture devices lack protection and hard-hat compatibility, barring many industrial sites. reGoggles makes ego data capture possible in frontline industrial environments.

  • Hard-hat Compatibility

  • Passive Ego Data Capture

  • Modular Design for Adaptable Sensors

Designed For Industrial AI

With safety, data capture, and hands-free use at its core, reGoggles fits seamlessly into the industrial intelligence systems of enterprises and developers alike.

Goggle-Form Capture and Protection

Designed to replace standard safety goggles — protection and capture in one device.

Modular Expansion

Two open expansion ports for additional cameras, TOF depth sensors, IMUs, or custom modules.

Hands-Free Interaction

3 mics and 2 speakers for voice commands and audio feedback — hands-free, eyes on the job.

Open Firmware and SDK

Built on the open Allwinner V881 platform — free to develop your own custom apps without lock-in.

Synchronized Multimodal Capture

Video, audio, and IMU data streams on one synced timeline.

Universal Connectivity

Standard Wi-Fi and BLE interfaces transmit data to your edge AI, cloud AI, and other applications.
